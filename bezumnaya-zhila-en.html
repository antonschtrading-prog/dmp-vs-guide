🌋 Mad Vein

Z Route: Redemption · DMP field guide · 17 November 2026

Goal: mine yttrium ore continuously, finish collections and deliver the ore to your base while avoiding unnecessary troop losses. There are two competitive rounds of 30 minutes each. Check the event screen for your actual start times.

Finishing a collection matters: you receive the completion bonus, and delivered unprocessed ore or crystals keep earning coins every second.

2. Preparation

  • Check both round times, participating servers, chest thresholds and rewards.
  • Agree on a gathering area with DMP. Prepare squads, hospital capacity and healing speedups.
  • During the preview stage, do not fight over tiles for event points. Competitive mining has not started.
  • Wait for the active round before entering the central danger zone. Keep your squads and teleport ready.

3. The 30-minute round

TimeAction
0–5 minTeleport, choose reachable deposits, deploy squads immediately.
5–15 minAvoid idle squads, finish mining, deliver ore, watch enemy approaches.
15–25 minBuild passive ore income and avoid fights you cannot win.
25–30 minFinish available collections; do not sacrifice troops for a small point gain.

Choose deposits by power: smaller players focus on relatively safe 40/sec deposits, mid-power players on reliable 80/sec, and strong players may contest 160/sec crystals near the center. Completing an 80/sec deposit is often better than repeatedly losing a 160/sec fight.

Watch incoming marches. If a stronger player attacks your deposit, recall the squad and move to another target. You can contest an opponent’s nearly finished deposit in battle, but plan around the remaining ore rather than assuming all previously earned coins transfer. Do not attack DMP members or friendly alliances without coordination.

4. Base, garrison and volcano

If your base is breached, part of the stored yttrium ore is lost, but coins already earned are kept.

The “Join Defense” checkboxes determine which squads protect your base wall. Against an overwhelmingly stronger enemy, you can remove squads from wall defense to avoid wasting troops in a hopeless fight. The trade-off is an easier breach and potential loss of stored ore. Restore your normal garrison settings after the event.

Meteors and shields: falling fragments can damage bases during the round. Do not rely on a regular shield in the central danger zone. Monitor base health and the hospital; adjust garrison settings primarily for player attacks.

5. Rewards

Event coins unlock reward chests. Personal and alliance ranking rewards are also available. Check the current event screen before each round: chest counts and thresholds may vary between event versions.

7. Responding to threats

  • Enemy approaching a deposit: compare strength and arrival time; recall if you cannot win.
  • Enemy near the base: assess garrison, stored ore and retreat options. Track teleport cooldown.
  • Base damaged: check fire, durability and hospital; repair and heal.
  • Squad becomes available: send it to a new deposit right away. Watch markers for falling fragments.

8. Team coordination

Strong players deter rivals; mid-power players maintain steady income; newer players farm less-contested deposits. Share gathering coordinates, enemy threats and new deposits in clan chat. Do not crowd everyone into one high-risk area without a plan.

Before round two: heal troops, rebuild squads, check the timer and assess the next event zone.

9. DMP quick checklist

Check timer → gather the group → occupy a deposit → finish mining → deliver ore → repeat → protect troops and coins.

Avoid: fighting before the start, idle squads, chasing 160/sec at any cost, forgetting garrison checkboxes and relying on outdated event times.
